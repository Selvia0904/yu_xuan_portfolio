Forword

FORWORD leverages AI-generated imagery to combat vocabulary forgetfulness. By transforming rote memorization into a visual experience, it makes language learning both highly efficient and engaging.

Transforming rote memorization into sensory experience

The name Forword—a blend of "Word" and "Forward"—reflects my core belief: that every word mastered is a step toward a student's goals.

FORWORD harnesses generative AI to bridge the gap between abstract vocabulary and long-term retention. By transforming student-written sentences into personalized visual stimuli, the platform turns static word lists into emotionally resonant experiences.

Preliminary Research

Introduction

Through interviews with 10 diverse learners, I replaced assumptions with authentic insights, uncovering the real-world pain points that drive effective vocabulary acquisition.

Problem 1

Scattered Resources

"I write words in my notebook, on sticky notes, and in my phone; when it's time to study, I can never find everything."

— Participant 3
Problem 2

High Entry Barrier

"I spend more time typing than studying. Sometimes I skip adding words simply because the process takes too long."

— Participant 8
Problem 3

Lack of Context

"Memorizing word lists feels like cramming phone numbers—they don't stick in my mind without a real connection."

— Participant 7

Target Audience

Digital Natives

For high schoolers raised on short-form content, traditional learning often fails to hold focus. FORWORD addresses this by providing the interactive, visually stimulating, and personalized experiences these students need to stay engaged.

Design Strategy

Instant Gratification

By streamlining input through OCR scanning, we minimize cognitive overload. This allows students to focus on the "High Reward"—instant AI-generated imagery that transforms rote memorization into a dopamine-driven creative loop.

Color Palette

Psychology of Blue

I chose Sky Blue based on Takashi Ishii's theory that blue acts as a catalyst for memory. Known as a "Connecting Color," it fosters neural associations and mental clarity, making the UI itself a tool for better information retention.
